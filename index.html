VRun

An immersive experimental project exploring rythm, movement, voice and embodied interaction through collective digital environments.

About the project

VRun is a collaborative research-creation project developed between France and New Zealand. The project investigates how rhythm, bodily presence, voice and collective interaction can shape immersive environments and sensitive digital experiences.

EMIKA

Emika live on tour. If We Disappear is an immersive acoustic-visual journey crafted by multimedia artist and sound designer Emika. Set against the backdrop of her album Falling In Love With Sadness, this live show is designed for the planetarium, combining voice, piano, hypnotic beats and 360° visuals.
